# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads translate product and project priorities into technical execution plans. They align implementation decisions, manage delivery dependencies, and help the team move from discovery to shipping work reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Own technical execution for key workstreams and milestones
- Coordinate engineering planning, sequencing, and dependency management
- Support architecture and design decisions with product and project stakeholders
- Help unblock delivery issues and escalate cross-team dependencies early
- Partner with PMs and developers to ensure feasibility, quality, and timelines are realistic

### Goals
- Deliver technical solutions aligned to product goals
- Improve predictability and flow across engineering work
- Reduce bottlenecks and maintain a healthy delivery rhythm

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and roadmap discussions
- Dependency reviews with PMs and cross-functional partners
- Design reviews, technical trade-off discussions, and delivery updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers on implementation choices and execution trade-offs
- Aligns with Product Managers on scope, sequencing, and feasibility
- Reports status and risks to Project Managers for planning and stakeholder communication

---

## Security & Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security & Compliance Partners ensure that project work meets organizational standards for privacy, risk, and security controls. They help identify and reduce risk without slowing team delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Review project scope for security, privacy, and compliance considerations
- Assess risks, controls, and dependencies that affect delivery or launch readiness
- Support secure design decisions and incident response planning
- Review release risk and ensure remediation plans are in place before go-live
- Collaborate with PMs and engineering leads on policy and audit requirements

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across project work
- Maintain trust and policy compliance without unnecessary delays
- Ensure launch readiness and ongoing operational safety

### Typical Communication
- Risk workshops and compliance reviews
- Security sign-off checkpoints during planning and release readiness
- Incident escalation and mitigation follow-ups

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Engineering Leads to vet technical designs and release readiness
- Advises Product Managers on risk trade-offs and constraints
- Supports Project Managers in mitigation planning and escalation paths

---

## Data & Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data & Analytics Leads define the evidence and measurement plan for the project. They help connect delivery work to business outcomes and support data-informed decision-making.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, instrumentation needs, and reporting baselines
- Monitor product and operational performance linked to project outcomes
- Support experiments, analysis, and decision-making with clear metrics
- Partner with stakeholders to interpret trends and validate business impact
- Help translate business goals into measurable delivery signals

### Goals
- Improve decision quality through measurable evidence
- Ensure insights are tracked from early discovery through release
- Support continuous improvement using outcome-based metrics

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews and milestone reporting
- Stakeholder updates on product health and performance trends
- Data readiness and reporting discussions with PMs and Product Managers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate if outcomes are being achieved
- Helps Engineering Leads define observability and system metrics for rollout and support
- Provides status and trends that Project Managers can communicate to stakeholders

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Stakeholder Liaisons maintain alignment between the team and the people affected by the project. They gather feedback, support adoption, and help translate stakeholder needs into execution priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain regular communication with stakeholders, customers, and internal partners
- Capture feedback, requests, and operational concerns that affect project priorities
- Support rollout readiness, adoption planning, and stakeholder expectations
- Identify risks related to user experience, change management, or adoption barriers
- Coordinate communication and feedback loops between delivery teams and stakeholders

### Goals
- Improve customer and stakeholder alignment throughout delivery
- Reduce surprises during releases and milestones
- Support adoption and successful project outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and readiness reviews
- Customer feedback sessions and adoption check-ins
- Release communications and follow-up discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Feeds stakeholder needs and customer feedback into Product Manager prioritization
- Helps Project Managers keep communication plans accurate and current
- Provides context for Engineering Leads and Developers on user pain points and rollout concerns

---

## Release Managers / Enablement Partners

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate launch readiness, communication, and operational support for new features or changes. They help ensure that releases happen with clear ownership, validation, and contingency planning.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release schedules, dependencies, and launch readiness reviews
- Maintain release checklists, deployment plans, and rollback considerations
- Align communication plans across teams, stakeholders, and support channels
- Partner with QA and engineering leads to confirm release quality and impact assessment
- Support post-release validation and communication of known issues

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and improve delivery confidence
- Ensure stakeholders understand what is changing and when
- Support smooth adoption and rapid issue response after launch

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and launch check-ins
- Deployment and rollback planning discussions
- Post-release updates and stakeholder communications

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Engineering Leads on release sequencing and deployment confidence
- Collaborates with Project Managers on milestone and stakeholder communication plans
- Coordinates with Product Managers and Customer Success partners on go-live readiness and adoption support

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles help describe the cross-functional accountability that supports strong execution, stakeholder clarity, and safer delivery in real projects.
